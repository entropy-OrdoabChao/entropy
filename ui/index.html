A defect is a place where a filing or an order breaks a rule that binds it. Open one to see the rule, the ladder it comes from (Constitution down to the maxims of equity), and what it lets you do.

Cites a trial-court case as if it bound the appeals court

Answering brief · 3d Cir. · page 14 · by Troutman Pepper for Example Lending
Fatal
What happened
"…as this Court has held, a consumer who does not use the word 'dispute' has not disputed. Pasquariello v. Manwiller, No. C-48-CV-2020-00607 (Pa. Com. Pl. 2021)."
That case is from a county trial court in Pennsylvania. It cannot bind the Third Circuit, and it is not about the federal statute.
Which rule it breaks
Stare decisis: only the Supreme Court and the Third Circuit bind this court on federal law. Counsel also owes candor about what authority is. Model Rule 3.3(a)(2); Pa. R.P.C. 3.3
Where the rule comes from
ConstitutionArt. III: one Supreme Court and the inferior courts Congress establishes; the hierarchy is constitutional.
Public law · code28 U.S.C. §§ 41, 1291 (Title 28 is positive law, enacted 1948): the Third Circuit hears appeals from E.D. Pa.
Regulationnone applies
Court rule3d Cir. I.O.P. 9.1: a panel is bound by prior panel and Supreme Court decisions.
Canon · dutyCandor toward the tribunal (Rule 3.3): a lawyer must not misstate the law.
Maxim · equityNemo ex suo delicto meliorem suam conditionem facere potest: no one may improve his position by his own wrong (Bouvier). A misstated authority earns nothing.
What it means for you
Your reply says so in one paragraph and asks the court to disregard the cite. Already in your reply, section III
Learn this
Binding vs. persuasive. A court must follow decisions of the courts above it. Everything else is at most persuasive. When a lawyer writes "this Court has held" about a lower court's case, the claim itself is false, and courts notice.

Order granted a motion without stating any reasons

Order on docket 38 · E.D. Pa. · page 1, lines 22 to 25 · by the court
Serious
What happened
"…it is hereby ORDERED that the Motion is GRANTED. The Plaintiff's Amended Complaint is DISMISSED."
No findings of fact, no statement of which element failed.
Which rule it breaks
Fed. R. Civ. P. 52(a) and the due-process requirement of a reasoned decision.
Where the rule comes from
ConstitutionAmend. V and XIV: due process requires a decision a person can understand and appeal.
Public law · code28 U.S.C. § 2072 (Rules Enabling Act) gives the civil rules the force of law.
Regulationnone applies
Court ruleFed. R. Civ. P. 52(a)(1): the court "must find the facts specially and state its conclusions of law separately."
Canon · policy"Must" is mandatory; Code of Conduct for U.S. Judges, Canon 3(A)(4).
Maxim · equityAudi alteram partem: hear the other side; and equity will not suffer a wrong to be without a remedy. A ruling with no reasons denies both (Black's; Bouvier).
What it means for you
A motion for a statement of reasons under Rule 52(a); the missing reasons are your first argument on appeal. Draft ready
Learn this
Reasons are part of the ruling. An order that gives none leaves you nothing to answer and the appeals court nothing to review. Asking for reasons is normal and the rule requires them.

Motion to Strike relies on facts outside the complaint

Motion to Strike · E.D. Pa. docket 41 · pages 3 to 5 · by Troutman Pepper for Example Lending
Serious
Which rule it breaks
Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(f) reaches only what is on the face of the pleading; account records are outside it. If the court considers them, Rule 12(d) converts the motion to summary judgment and you get to respond with evidence.

Reply Brief of Appellant

I. Example Lending never answers the actual question. Example Lending's brief spends eleven pages on whether the filer used the word "dispute." The statute does not require that word. It requires a written notice that the debt is disputed, and the June 4 letter says the balance "is not correct and I do not owe it." 15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b); Graziano v. Harrison, 950 F.2d 107, 112 (3d Cir. 1991).

II. The district court decided facts on a motion to dismiss. The order treated Example Lending's account history as true and the filer's letter as unproven. At the pleading stage the court must do the opposite. Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(d); Phillips v. County of Allegheny, 515 F.3d 224, 231 (3d Cir. 2008).

The gate, every time
1. Where is it from? An official site, a court, an official reporter, or a scan of the official print.
2. Is it law as written, or commentary about law?

What passes goes where
Law as written → your law library, with a source anchor, searchable and citable.
Official guidance, treatises → library, marked secondary, cited as [official] or [doc].
Everything else → a note in your Obsidian, marked unverified. Never cited, never used to draft.
Why so strict
A filing is only as strong as its weakest cite. The desk teaches the difference between what the law says and what someone says about it, by making you prove it each time.
