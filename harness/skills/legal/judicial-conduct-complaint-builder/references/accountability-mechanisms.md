# Judicial Accountability Mechanisms

**Updated: June 20, 2026**

## Federal Judicial Misconduct — Third Circuit

- **Form**: AO 310 (Rev. 03/16)
- **Form URL**: https://www.ca3.uscourts.gov/sites/ca3/files/FormalComplaint.Appendix3.pdf
- **Mailing**: Circuit Executive, Third Circuit, 22409 U.S. Courthouse, 601 Market Street, Philadelphia, PA 19106-1790
- **Envelope**: "COMPLAINT OF MISCONDUCT" (DO NOT name judge on envelope)
- **Fee**: $0 | **Deadline**: Reasonable time | **Review**: ~6 months
- **Remedies**: Private admonishment, public reprimand, impeachment referral, voluntary retirement
- **NOT available**: Monetary damages, vacatur, case reopening
- **Escalation**: Rule 21 to Judicial Conference Committee (63 days from JC decision)

## State Judicial Misconduct — Pennsylvania

- **Mailing**: Judicial Conduct Board, 601 Commonwealth Ave, Suite 3500, Harrisburg, PA 17106-2525
- **Phone**: (717) 234-7911
- **Form URL**: https://judicialconductboardofpa.org/filing-a-complaint/
- **Anonymous**: Accepted
- **Remedies**: Private admonishment, public reprimand, censure, suspension, removal
- **NOT available**: Monetary damages, vacatur, case reopening

## Mandamus Against Federal Judges

- **Authority**: 28 U.S.C. § 1651 + FRAP 21
- **Where**: Third Circuit Court of Appeals
- **Standard**: Cheney v. U.S. Dist. Court, 542 U.S. 367 (2004)
  1. Clear and indisputable right
  2. No other adequate remedy
  3. Appropriate under the circumstances

## Section 1983 (State Judges Only)

- **SOL**: 2 years (42 Pa.C.S. § 5524)
- **Where**: U.S. District Court (any federal venue with personal jurisdiction)
- **Fee**: $405 (waivable via IFP)

## Judicial Recusal

- **28 U.S.C. § 455**: "impartiality might reasonably be questioned"
- **Caperton v. A.T. Massey Coal Co., 556 U.S. 868 (2009)**: probability of bias
- **Liteky v. United States, 510 U.S. 540 (1994)**: extreme departure from the norm

## Immunity Exceptions

| Exception | Source | Applicable? |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
