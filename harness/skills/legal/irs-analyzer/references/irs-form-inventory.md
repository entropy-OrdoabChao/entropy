# Complete IRS Form Inventory

## Debt Cancellation Forms

### Form 1099-C — Cancellation of Debt
- **26 U.S.C. § 6050P** — Any person who is required to file a return under
  § 6041 or § 6041A and who cancels or forgives a debt of $600 or more
  must file Form 1099-C with the IRS and furnish a copy to the debtor.
- **26 U.S.C. § 108** — Exclusion from gross income for discharge of indebtedness.
  A debtor may exclude canceled debt from gross income if:
  - The debt is discharged in a Title 11 bankruptcy case
  - The debt is qualified farm indebtedness
  - The debt is qualified business indebtedness
  - The debt is discharged when the debtor is insolvent
  - The debt is qualified principal residence indebtedness

### Form 1099-A — Acquisition or Abandonment of Secured Property
- **26 U.S.C. § 6050F** — Returns relating to acquisition or abandonment of
  secured property. Any person who acquires or abandons secured property in
  satisfaction of an obligation to pay or redeem or in foreclosure must file
  a return.

## Payment Forms

### Form 1099-K — Payment Card and Third-Party Network Transactions
- **26 U.S.C. § 6050W** — Returns relating to payments made in settlement of
  card and third-party network transactions.

### Form 1099-MISC — Miscellaneous Income
- **26 U.S.C. § 6041** — Returns relating to receipts of $600 or more.
  Any person engaged in a trade or business who makes payments of $600 or more
  to another person for rent, services, prizes, awards, other income, royalties,
  farm rents must file a return.

### Form 1099-INT — Interest Income
- **26 U.S.C. § 6049** — Returns relating to interest paid. Any person who is
  required to file a return under § 6041 or § 6041A and who pays interest of
  $10 or more must file a return.
