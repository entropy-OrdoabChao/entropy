# Jurisdictional Hat-Swapping Detector

## Overview

Judges sometimes apply the wrong jurisdictional framework — maritime law
when they should apply Article 3, Article 1 when they should apply Article 3,
etc. This skill detects those hat-swaps and builds challenges.

## Key Hat-Swap Patterns

### Article 1 vs. Article 3

- Article 1 judges (bankruptcy, tax, etc.) cannot exercise Article 3 judicial
  power
- Article 3 judges have life tenure and cannot be removed by Congress
- If an Article 1 judge issues an order that requires Article 3 judicial power,
  it is void

### Maritime vs. Federal

- Maritime law applies to admiralty and maritime cases
- Federal courts have admiralty jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. 1333
- If a judge applies maritime law to a non-maritime case, the order is void

### Federal vs. State

- Federal courts cannot issue orders that bind state courts
- State courts cannot issue orders that bind federal courts
- If a federal judge issues an order that purports to bind a state court,
  the order is void

### Supplemental vs. Original Jurisdiction

- Supplemental jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. 1367 is limited
- If a judge applies supplemental jurisdiction beyond its scope, the order
  is void

### Step 1: Query the corpus for the jurisdictional framework

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=JURISDICTIONAL+FRAMEWORK+statute+text&top_k=10

### Step 2: Cross-reference with Beezle MCP

- mcp_beezle_jurisdiction_check — check subject matter and personal jurisdiction
- mcp_beezle_analyze_construction — apply rules of statutory construction
- mcp_beezle_check_definition_scope — classify a statutory definition

### Step 3: Build the challenge

For each hat-swap found, document:
- The hat-swap (what the judge did wrong)
- The correct jurisdictional framework (what the judge should have done)
- The citation (which statute or precedent establishes the correct framework)
- The remedy (motion to vacate, motion to dismiss, etc.)

### Step 4: Write to Obsidian

Write the challenge to Obsidian:
    write_obsidian_note(subdir=SUBDIR, filename=FILENAME, content=CONTENT)

### Step 5: Add wikilinks

## Key patterns

### Article 1 judge exercising Article 3 power

1. GET /search_text?query=Article+1+judge+Article+3+judicial+power
2. Document the hat-swap
3. Build the challenge: Article 1 judge cannot exercise Article 3 power
4. Write to Obsidian: jurisdictional_hat_swap_article1_vs_article3.md

### Maritime law applied to non-maritime case

1. GET /search_text?query=maritime+law+non-maritime+case
2. Document the hat-swap
3. Build the challenge: Maritime law does not apply to non-maritime cases
4. Write to Obsidian: jurisdictional_hat_swap_maritime_vs_federal.md

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume a judge is applying the wrong framework — verify the law
- Don't assume a judge is beyond jurisdiction — verify the jurisdiction
- Don't assume a hat-swap is real — verify the facts
- Don't build a challenge without documenting the correct framework
