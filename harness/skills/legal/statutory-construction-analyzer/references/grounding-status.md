# Grounding Status (verified 2026-07-22)

## Canons of Statutory Construction — NOT GROUNDED IN THE CORPUS

The canons listed in this skill (expressio unius, ejusdem generis, last antecedent, mutual exclusion,
surplusage) are general legal canons — they are correct in principle but **NOT GROUNDED IN THE CORPUS**.
The corpus contains actual statutory text and case law, not treatise-level discussions of canons.

When using these canons:
1. Ground the specific statutory text via `search_text` and cite the actual statute
2. Do NOT cite the canon itself as authority — the canon is an interpretive tool, not legal authority
3. The corpus can only confirm the actual text; it cannot confirm the canon's application
