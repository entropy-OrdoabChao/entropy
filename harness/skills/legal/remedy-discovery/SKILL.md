# Remedy Discovery

## Overview

Every statute has remedies — sometimes multiple, sometimes layered. This skill
ensures you find ALL remedy avenues, not just the obvious ones.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Query the corpus for the statute + remedy keywords

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=STATUTE+damages+remedy&top_k=10

Try multiple keyword combinations:
- STATUTE + damages
- STATUTE + injunctive relief
- STATUTE + fees
- STATUTE + punitive damages
- STATUTE + attorneys fees

### Step 2: Query the corpus for the statute + enforcement keywords

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=STATUTE+enforcement+provision&top_k=10

### Step 3: Query the corpus for the statute + penalty keywords

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=STATUTE+penalty+civil&top_k=10

### Step 4: Cross-reference with MCP tools

- `mcp_beezle_analyze_remedies` — map available remedies (damages, injunction, fees)
  and whether mandatory or discretionary
- `mcp_beezle_build_logic_chain` — build IF/AND/THEN chains for each remedy

### Step 5: Build the remedy matrix

For each remedy found, document:
- Remedy type (damages, injunction, fees, etc.)
- Statutory basis (which statute/section)
- Mandatory or discretionary
- Elements required
- Available damages amounts (if any)
- Any caps or limitations

## Key patterns

### Statutory damages

- Fixed amounts per violation (e.g., FCRA: $100-$1,000 per violation)
- Often in addition to actual damages

### Punitive damages

- May require showing malice, fraud, or willful disregard
- Often subject to constitutional limits (due process)

### Attorney fees

- May be mandatory (if prevailing party) or discretionary
- May require showing bad faith or frivolousness

### Injunctive relief

- May be mandatory or equitable
- Often requires showing irreparable harm and inadequacy of legal remedy

## Example: FCRA remedies

1. GET /search_text?query=FCRA 15 USC 1681 damages remedy
2. GET /search_text?query=FCRA 15 USC 1681 injunctive relief
3. GET /search_text?query=FCRA 15 USC 1681 attorney fees
4. GET /search_text?query=FCRA 15 USC 1681 punitive damages
5. GET /search_text?query=FCRA 15 USC 1681 penalty provision

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume a statute has no damages — even if not explicitly stated, common
  law may provide remedies
- Don't forget equitable remedies — injunctions, declaratory judgments
- Don't forget attorney fees — often overlooked but can be substantial
- Don't assume statutory damages are the maximum — actual damages may be higher
- Check for cross-statute remedies — violating one statute may trigger another
