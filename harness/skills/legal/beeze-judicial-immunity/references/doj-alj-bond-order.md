# DOJ ALJ Bond Order

## Overview
The Department of Justice has issued an order requiring Administrative Law Judges (ALJs) to have a bond.

## Significance
This is the order the user referenced regarding the DOJ's position on judicial protection. The DOJ's position is that ALJs should not have the same immunity protections as Article III judges.
