# Unsealed Terms Pattern

When building a definition for a legal term and the term is NOT separately defined in the target regulation/statute, its scope is governed by the controlling definition of the bounding term.

## Pattern
1. Identify the bounding term (the term that defines the category the target term belongs to)
2. Find the controlling definition of that bounding term
3. The target term's scope = the scope of the bounding term's definition

## Example
**Target term:** "fiduciary activities" — not separately defined in 12 C.F.R. Part 9.
**Bounding term:** "fiduciary capacity" — defined in § 9.2(e).
**Result:** Fiduciary activities = all acts a national bank performs when acting in any capacity defined as fiduciary under § 9.2(e), not merely the enumerated trustee/executor/guardian roles.

## How to detect
- Search the target statute/regulation for the term — if it appears only in section titles, purpose statements, or references to other sections, it is NOT separately defined.
- Look for a "Definitions" section (§ 9.2 in Part 9) — if the term is NOT listed there, it is unsealed.
