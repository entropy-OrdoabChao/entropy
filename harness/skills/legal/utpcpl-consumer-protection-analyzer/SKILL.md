# UTPCPL Consumer Protection Analyzer

## Overview

The Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (UTPCPL) provides a
private right of action for consumers who are injured by unfair or deceptive
trade practices. This skill builds those claims.

## Legal Framework

### UTPCPL (73 Pa. C.S. 201-201.1)

UTPCPL prohibits unfair or deceptive practices in the conduct of any trade or
commerce. It provides a private right of action for consumers who are injured
by such practices.

### Elements of a UTPCPL Claim

1. The defendant engaged in an unfair or deceptive practice
2. The practice occurred in the conduct of a trade or commerce
3. The plaintiff was injured as a result
4. The injury was caused by the practice

### Damages Under UTPCPL

- Actual damages
- Treble damages (3x actual damages) if the practice was willful
- Attorney fees and costs

### Step 3: Build the claim

For each violation, document:
- The unfair or deceptive practice (what opposing counsel did)
- The trade or commerce connection (how it relates to the case)
- The injury (what harm resulted)
- The causation (how the practice caused the injury)
- The damages (actual damages, treble damages, attorney fees)

## Key patterns

### Unfair practice

1. GET /search_text?query=UTPCPL+unfair+practice
2. Document the unfair practice
3. Build the claim: Opposing counsel engaged in an unfair practice
4. Write to Obsidian: utpcpl_opposing_counsel_unfair.md

### Deceptive practice

1. GET /search_text?query=UTPCPL+deceptive+practice
2. Document the deceptive practice
3. Build the claim: Opposing counsel engaged in a deceptive practice
4. Write to Obsidian: utpcpl_opposing_counsel_deceptive.md

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume a practice is unfair or deceptive — verify the law
- Don't assume a practice is in the conduct of trade or commerce — verify the connection
- Don't assume a practice caused injury — verify the causation
- Don't build a claim without documenting the damages
- Don't build a claim without documenting the treble damages eligibility
