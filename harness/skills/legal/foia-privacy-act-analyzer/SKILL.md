# FOIA and Privacy Act Analyzer

## Overview

The Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and the Privacy Act provide a private
right of action for individuals who are denied access to government records.
This skill builds those claims.

## Legal Framework

### FOIA (5 U.S.C. 552)

FOIA requires federal agencies to disclose records upon request, unless the
records fall under one of nine exemptions. Agencies must respond within 20
business days.

### Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. 552a)

The Privacy Act requires federal agencies to maintain records about individuals
in a manner that ensures accuracy, relevance, timeliness, and completeness.
Individuals have the right to access their own records.

### Elements of a FOIA Claim

1. The agency failed to comply with a FOIA request
2. The request was made in accordance with FOIA
3. The agency failed to respond within the required timeframe
4. The agency failed to provide an exemption justification

### Elements of a Privacy Act Claim

1. The agency maintained a record about the individual
2. The record was not accurate, relevant, timely, or complete
3. The individual was denied access to the record
4. The agency failed to correct the record

### Damages Under FOIA

- Actual damages
- Reasonable attorney fees and costs
- In some cases, punitive damages for willful violations

### Damages Under the Privacy Act

- Actual damages
- Statutory damages of $1,000-$5,000 for willful violations
- Reasonable attorney fees and costs

### Step 3: Build the claim

For each violation, document:
- The agency (which agency failed to comply)
- The request (what was requested)
- The failure (what the agency failed to do)
- The applicable exemption (if any)
- The damages (actual damages, statutory damages, attorney fees)

## Key patterns

### FOIA failure to respond

1. GET /search_text?query=FOIA+agency+failure+response
2. Document the failure to respond
3. Build the claim: Agency failed to respond within 20 business days
4. Write to Obsidian: foia_agency_failure_to_respond.md

### Privacy Act inaccuracy

1. GET /search_text?query=Privacy+Act+inaccuracy+record
2. Document the inaccuracy
3. Build the claim: Agency maintained an inaccurate record
4. Write to Obsidian: privacy_act_inaccuracy.md

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume an agency is failing to comply — verify the request and response
- Don't assume an exemption applies — verify the exemption
- Don't assume a record is inaccurate — verify the record
- Don't build a claim without documenting the damages
- Don't build a claim without documenting the applicable exemption
