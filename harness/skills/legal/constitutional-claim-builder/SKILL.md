# Constitutional Claim Builder

## Overview

Constitutional claims are the strongest claims — they trump all other claims.
This skill builds constitutional claims at any level.

## Legal Framework

### Due Process (5th and 14th Amendments)

- The 5th Amendment applies to the federal government
- The 14th Amendment applies to the states
- Due process requires notice and an opportunity to be heard

### Equal Protection (14th Amendment)

- The Equal Protection Clause requires that similarly situated persons be treated
  equally
- If a judge treats a pro se litigant differently than a represented litigant,
  the treatment may violate equal protection

### First Amendment

- The First Amendment protects the right to speak and to petition the government
- If a judge punishes a litigant for speaking or petitioning, the treatment
  may violate the First Amendment

### Article III

- Article III establishes the judicial power of the United States
- If an Article 1 judge exercises Article 3 judicial power, the exercise is void

## Workflow

### Step 1: Query the corpus for the constitutional violation

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=CONSTITUTIONAL+VIOLATION+statute+text&top_k=10

### Step 2: Cross-reference with Beezle MCP

- mcp_beezle_analyze_immunity — analyze immunity status for a government defendant
- mcp_beezle_analyze_burden — analyze burden of proof for the claim
- mcp_beezle_analyze_remedies — map available remedies for the claim

### Step 3: Build the claim

For each constitutional violation, document:
- The constitutional provision violated (which amendment or article)
- The violation (what was violated)
- The citation (which precedent or statute establishes the violation)
- The damages (what harm resulted from the violation)

### Step 4: Write to Obsidian

Write the claim to Obsidian:
    write_obsidian_note(subdir=SUBDIR, filename=FILENAME, content=CONTENT)

### Step 5: Add wikilinks

Add wikilinks to the claim:
    obsidian_propose_link(from_note=CLAIM_NOTE, to_note=CASE_NOTE, edge_type=authority, citation=CITATION)

1. GET /search_text?query=due+process+violation+statute
2. Document the violation
3. Build the claim: Due process violation — no notice or opportunity to be heard

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume a constitutional violation has occurred — verify the facts
- Don't assume a constitutional violation is real — verify the law
- Don't assume a violation is void — verify the consequences
- Don't build a claim without documenting the damages
