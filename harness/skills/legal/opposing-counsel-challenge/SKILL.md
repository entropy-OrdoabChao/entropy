# Opposing Counsel Challenge

## Overview

Opposing counsel's filings are full of errors — procedural defects, authority
violations, and misrepresentations. This skill finds those errors systematically.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Query the corpus for the statute cited by opposing counsel

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=OPPOSING+STATUTE+actual+text&top_k=10

### Step 2: Query the corpus for the citation cited by opposing counsel

    GET http://localhost:8100/search_text?query=CITATION+binding+precedent&top_k=10

### Step 3: Cross-reference with Beezle MCP

- `mcp_beezle_challenge_opponent` — instant counter-strategy from audit data
- `mcp_beezle_classify_citation` — classify a citation by its issuing court
- `mcp_beezle_is_binding_on_court` — determine whether a cited court binds
  a target court under stare decisis
- `mcp_beezle_analyze_immunity` — analyze immunity status for a government
  defendant

### Step 4: Build the challenge

For each defect found, document:
- The defect (procedural, authority, substantive)
- The authority that establishes the defect
- The remedy (motion to strike, motion to dismiss, etc.)

## Key patterns

### Procedural defects

- Missing verification (when required)
- Missing certificate of service
- Missing proof of service
- Missing cover sheet
- Missing page limit compliance

### Authority violations

- Citing non-binding authority as controlling
- Citing overruled authority
- Citing authority from a different jurisdiction
- Citing authority that does not support the proposition

### Substantive defects

- Misquoting a statute
- Misinterpreting a statute
- Misapplying a case
- Failing to cite controlling authority

## Example: Opposing counsel cites 988 F.2d 476 in EDPA

1. GET /search_text?query=988 F.2d 476 binding precedent
2. Classify: 988 F.2d 476 = 9th Circuit (not binding on EDPA)
3. Query corpus for EDPA binding authority:
   GET /search_text?query=EDPA FCRA binding precedent
4. Challenge: Opposing counsel is citing 9th Circuit authority as controlling
   in EDPA — this is a fatal authority violation
5. File motion to strike the citation

## Pitfalls

- Don't accept opposing counsel's citations at face value — verify them
- Don't assume a citation is binding — verify the court hierarchy
- Don't assume a statute is being cited correctly — verify the text
- When in doubt, query the corpus directly rather than relying on MCP tool
  defaults
