# Bonds and Insurance Remedy

## Overview

Judges and courts are required to post bonds and maintain insurance. When they
fail to do so, there are financial remedies available. This skill builds those
claims.

## Legal Framework

### Judicial Bonds

- Federal judges are required to post bonds under 28 U.S.C. 333
- State judges are required to post bonds under state law
- If a judge fails to post a bond, the bond is forfeited

### Court Insurance

- Courts are required to maintain insurance for certain claims
- If a court fails to maintain insurance, the insurance is forfeited

### Financial Remedies

- Forfeiture of the bond
- Forfeiture of the insurance
- Damages for the failure to post a bond or maintain insurance

### Step 3: Build the claim

For each failure to post a bond or maintain insurance, document:
- The entity (which judge or court failed to post a bond or maintain insurance)
- The requirement (what bond or insurance was required)
- The failure (what the entity failed to do)
- The citation (which statute or rule establishes the requirement)
- The remedy (forfeiture of the bond, forfeiture of the insurance, damages)

## Key patterns

### Judicial bond forfeiture

1. GET /search_text?query=judicial+bond+forfeiture
2. Document the failure to post a bond
3. Build the claim: Forfeiture of the judicial bond
4. Write to Obsidian: bonds_insurance_judicial_bond_forfeiture.md

### Court insurance forfeiture

1. GET /search_text?query=court+insurance+forfeiture
2. Document the failure to maintain insurance
3. Build the claim: Forfeiture of the court insurance
4. Write to Obsidian: bonds_insurance_court_insurance_forfeiture.md

## Pitfalls

- Don't assume a judge has failed to post a bond — verify the requirement
- Don't assume a court has failed to maintain insurance — verify the requirement
- Don't assume a bond is forfeited — verify the forfeiture rules
- Don't build a claim without documenting the requirement
