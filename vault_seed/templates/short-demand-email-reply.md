## Short Demand Email Reply

Use for a single wrong — one fact, one legal hook, one remedy, one deadline, one consequence. No filler.

```
[Subject line — brief, specific]

Dear [Party/Counsel Name],

1. [ONE concrete wrong — who did what, when, on what basis]. [Docket/Exhibit citation].

2. [Legal hook — binding rule + citation]. [Verbatim quote or precise paraphrase].

3. [Specific remedy demanded].

4. [Deadline — calendar date, not "within X days"].

5. [Consequence if ignored — next step, with citation to the authority that supports it].

[Your name/title]
```

### Rules

- ONE wrong only. If there are multiple wrongs, write separate letters.
- Every legal claim must be corpus-grounded. If search_chunks returns nothing,
  write `[CORPUS GROUNDING REQUIRED — verify before sending]` instead of guessing.
- Deadline must be a calendar date. No "within 30 days" — use "by [date]".
- Consequence must cite authority — not just "I will file suit."
- No qualifiers: "may," "might," "arguably," "it appears."
- No disclaimers or suggestions to consult counsel.

### When to use

- Email reply to opposing counsel or party
- Short-form demand (<=1 page)
- When a full demand letter would be overkill but you still need a grounded, enforceable notice

### When NOT to use

- Multiple distinct wrongs (write separate letters)
- Complex factual disputes requiring discovery
- When the opposing party is a government agency (use formal notice format instead)
