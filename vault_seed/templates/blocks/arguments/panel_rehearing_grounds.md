## GROUNDS FOR PANEL REHEARING

### 1. Standard

Panel rehearing is governed by Fed. R. App. P. 40. The petition must state with particularity each point of law or fact that the petitioner believes the Court has overlooked or misapprehended. Fed. R. App. P. 40(a)(2). Rehearing is not an occasion to reargue the merits; it is the vehicle to correct a demonstrable oversight or misapprehension that, if corrected, would change the outcome.
