## GROUNDS TO RECALL THE MANDATE

### 1. Standard

A court of appeals possesses inherent power to recall its mandate, but that power is to be exercised sparingly and only upon a showing of *exceptional circumstances*. *See* *Calderon v. Thompson*, 523 U.S. 538, 549-50 (1998); Fed. R. App. P. 41(d). Recall is warranted where the mandate (or, here, the certified order entered in lieu of a mandate under 3d Cir. L.A.R. 107.2(b)) issued on a premise the record does not support, or where issuance would work a manifest injustice that ordinary post-judgment process cannot correct.
