## GROUNDS FOR THE WRIT

### 1. Standard

Mandamus is a drastic remedy reserved for extraordinary circumstances. Three conditions must be satisfied: (1) the petitioner must have no other adequate means to attain the relief desired; (2) the petitioner must show that the right to issuance of the writ is clear and indisputable; and (3) the issuing court, in its discretion, must be satisfied that the writ is appropriate under the circumstances. *Cheney v. U.S. Dist. Court*, 542 U.S. 367, 380-81 (2004); *Kerr v. U.S. Dist. Court*, 426 U.S. 394, 403 (1976); Fed. R. App. P. 21.
