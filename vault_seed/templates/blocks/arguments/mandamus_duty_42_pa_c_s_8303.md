## MANDAMUS DUTY — 42 Pa.C.S. § 8303

### 1. The Statute

42 Pa.C.S. § 8303 provides: "A person who is adjudged in an action in the nature of mandamus to have failed or refused without lawful justification to perform a duty required by law shall be liable in damages to the person aggrieved by such failure or refusal."

This statute creates liability for any judge or court officer who fails to perform a duty required by law — including the duty to rule on pending motions within a reasonable time.

### 2. The Duty

The duty to rule on pending motions is required by:
- Pa.R.C.P. 1038 (mandatory 60-day hearing requirement)
- The Pennsylvania Constitution, Article V, § 1 (judicial duty to administer justice)
- Due process requirements under the Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments
