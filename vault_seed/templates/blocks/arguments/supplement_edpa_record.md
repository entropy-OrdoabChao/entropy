## ARGUMENT FOR SUPPLEMENTING THE EDPA RECORD

### 1. The EDPA Record Is Incomplete for Rule 60 Proceedings

The plaintiff's Rule 60 motions (dkt 56-60) reference exhibits that are not yet on the EDPA docket: GreenSky letters, correction docket entries, 1099-C forms, and other evidence. These documents are essential to the Rule 60 proceedings and must be part of the record.

### 2. Legal Authority

FRE 201(b)(2) authorizes the court to take judicial notice of official records. FRCP 60(a) authorizes correction of clerical errors in any record. The court has inherent authority to ensure the record is complete for the proceedings before it.

### 3. Application

The Rule 60 proceedings depend on evidence that is not on the docket. Without this evidence, the Court cannot properly evaluate the motions. The plaintiff has filed these exhibits separately and they should be incorporated into the record.

### 4. Relief Requested

Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court:
1. Supplement the record with the referenced exhibits;
2. Take judicial notice of the official records referenced;
3. Consider the Rule 60 motions with a complete record.
