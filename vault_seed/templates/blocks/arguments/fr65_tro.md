## Temporary Restraining Order Under FRCP 65

### A. The Standard — FRCP 65(b)

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 65(b) allows a TRO to be issued without notice only if:

1. Specific facts in an affidavit or verified complaint show that immediate and irreparable injury will result to the movant before the adverse party can be heard in opposition;
2. The movant's counsel certifies in writing the efforts made to give notice and the reasons for supporting the request without notice;
3. The movant or counsel provides a good-faith effort to notify the adverse party; and
4. The movant or counsel provides reasonable efforts to determine the identity of the adverse party's counsel of record.

### B. Duration — FRCP 65(b)(2)

A TRO issued without notice expires at the time after entry of the court sets, unless before that time the court, for good cause, extends it for a like period, or the adverse party consents to a longer extension.

### C. Elements — Irreparable Harm

To obtain a TRO, the movant must show:

1. **Immediate and irreparable injury**: The injury cannot be adequately remedied by money damages and will occur before the adverse party can be heard;
2. **Likelihood of success on the merits**: The movant has a reasonable probability of prevailing; and
3. **Balance of equities**: The threatened injury to the movant outweighs the harm the TRO would cause the opposing party.
