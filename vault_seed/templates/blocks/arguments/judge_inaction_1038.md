## JUDGE INACTION — Pa.R.C.P. 1038

### 1. The Rule

Pa.R.C.P. 1038 authorizes a party to move for an order compelling a judge to rule on a pending motion. Upon such motion, the court shall hear oral argument within 60 days after filing, with extensions available for discovery or good cause. This rule exists precisely to prevent procedural paralysis when a judge fails to act on a pending matter.

### 4. The Court's Duty

Under Pennsylvania law, a judge has a ministerial duty to rule on pending motions within a reasonable time. Pa.R.C.P. 1038 codifies this duty and provides the procedural mechanism to enforce it. The rule is not discretionary — "the court shall hear oral argument" is mandatory language.
