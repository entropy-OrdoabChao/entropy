## GROUNDS FOR REHEARING EN BANC

### 1. Standard

En banc rehearing is governed by Fed. R. App. P. 35. It is not favored and will ordinarily not be ordered unless (1) it is necessary to secure or maintain uniformity of the court's decisions, or (2) the proceeding involves a question of exceptional importance. Fed. R. App. P. 35(a). A petition must begin with a statement that the panel decision conflicts with a decision of the Supreme Court or of this Court (with citation), or that the proceeding involves a question of exceptional importance. Fed. R. App. P. 35(b)(1).
