## Judgment as a Matter of Law Under FRCP 50

### A. The Standard — FRCP 50(a)(1)

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 50(a)(1) permits a party to move for judgment as a matter of law at any time before the case is submitted to the jury, if the party has been fully heard on an issue and there is no legally sufficient evidentiary basis for a reasonable jury to find for that party on that issue.

### B. The Standard — FRCP 50(a)(2) — Renewal of JMOL

FRCP 50(a)(2) permits a party to renew the request for judgment as a matter of law — and to alternately request a new trial — by filing the motion within 28 days after the court enters judgment.

### C. The Standard — FRCP 50(b)

FRCP 50(b) requires that if the court does not grant a motion for judgment as a matter of law, the court must also grant a motion for a new trial if the court grants a motion for judgment as a matter of law that was filed under Rule 50(a)(1).
