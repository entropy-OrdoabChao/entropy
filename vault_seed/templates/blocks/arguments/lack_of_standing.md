## Opposing Party Lacks Article III / Statutory Standing

Standing is an indispensable part of a plaintiff's case. *Lujan v.
Defenders of Wildlife*, 504 U.S. 555, 561 (1992). The party
invoking jurisdiction bears the burden of establishing each
element. *Id.*

In **{{caption}}**, the record fails to establish standing as to
the relief opposing parties seek. Specifically, opposing parties
have not demonstrated (i) a concrete and particularized injury,
(ii) traceable to the challenged conduct, and (iii) redressable
by a favorable ruling. *See Spokeo, Inc. v. Robins*, 578 U.S. 330,
339 (2016); *TransUnion LLC v. Ramirez*, 594 U.S. 413 (2021).
Where standing is absent, the merits cannot be reached.
