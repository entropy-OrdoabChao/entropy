## New Trial Under FRCP 59

### A. The Standard — FRCP 59(a)

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 59(a) allows a new trial for any reason for which relief has been available at common law:

- The verdict is contrary to the weight of the evidence;
- The trial was unfair to the moving party or prejudiced the moving party's rights;
- The verdict is against the weight of the evidence; or
- Damages are excessive or inadequate.

### B. Time Limits — FRCP 59(b)

A motion for a new trial must be served no later than 28 days after the entry of the judgment.

### C. Application — Why a New Trial Is Warranted
