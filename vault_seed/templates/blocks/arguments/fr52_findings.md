## Findings of Fact Under FRCP 52

### A. The Standard — FRCP 52(a)

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 52(a) requires the court to find the facts specially and state its conclusions of law separately when it hears an equity case, or when a jury trial is waived:

- **For a bench trial**: The court must find the facts specially and state separately its conclusions of law. The findings may be stated in a written opinion, oral opinion, or order.
- **For a jury trial**: The court must submit interrogatories on the issues of fact and may render a general verdict.

The court's findings are not subject to review unless clearly erroneous. A finding is clearly erroneous when the reviewing court has a definite and firm conviction that a mistake has been made, even though there is evidence to support it.

### B. The Standard — FRCP 52(b) — Amendment of Findings

A court may amend its findings — and make additional findings — and may amend the judgment accordingly. The amendment may be made after giving notice and an opportunity to be heard.
