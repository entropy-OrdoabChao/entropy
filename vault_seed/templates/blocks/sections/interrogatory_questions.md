## II. QUESTIONS PRESENTED FOR THE COURT'S STATEMENT OF REASONS

To permit meaningful review and to confirm the Order rests on a reasoned application of binding law,
Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court answer each of the following. Each question is tied to a
specific controlling authority and/or record fact that the Order did not address.

[Generate ONE numbered, pointed question for EACH controlling authority and each material defect in the
scorecard that the Order failed to address (use as many as the record supports — do not cap artificially).
Each question must demand a legal or factual articulation and be framed so that any answer either grants
the relief or exposes a failure to apply binding law or to construe the pro se filing liberally. Pattern:
"Did the Court consider [exact controlling authority]? If so, how is the denial of [the specific motion/
argument] reconciled with [the holding]? If not, on what legal basis was [the controlling rule] set
aside?" Quote and cite each authority exactly from the scorecard / input. No rhetorical questions; no
invented authority; if a point is NOT IN the record or audit, omit it.]

Plaintiff requests that the Court's answers be set forth with specificity so the record reflects the
