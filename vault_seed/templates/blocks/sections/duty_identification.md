## VII. DUTY: WHO OWES THE STATUTORY DUTY

### A. The Rule

**IF** a statute or regulation imposes a duty on a defined class (e.g., furnisher, insurer, agency, trustee, financial agent, court officer),
**AND** the defendant falls within that defined class,
**THEN** the defendant owes the statutory duty.

The duty is defined by the statutory text, not by the defendant's character or capacity.

### B. Application: Duty Chain

**Duty 1:** The statute "{{statutory_duty_1}}" imposes a duty on "{{duty_holder_1}}" under **{{duty_authority_1}}**.

**Duty 2:** The statute "{{statutory_duty_2}}" imposes a duty on "{{duty_holder_2}}" under **{{duty_authority_2}}**.

**Duty 3:** The statute "{{statutory_duty_3}}" imposes a duty on "{{duty_holder_3}}" under **{{duty_authority_3}}**.

> **Y/N:** Does each defendant owe the duty identified above? The answer is **NO** if the defendant does not fall within the defined class.

### C. Defined-Term Chain: Duty Holder

The duty holder is defined by the statute. The chain must stay inside the SAME statute unless a cross-reference imports it.
