## NOTICE AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF OATH AND DUTY

This filing asks the Court and counsel to agree to **nothing new.** It restates the **oath each has
already taken and the Rules that already bind them,** and asks each to affirm they are following them
as to the matters raised. The undersigned respectfully requests that this acknowledgment be **attached
to the Court's order and to any response of counsel.** Each is free to decline; a ruling or response
that neither attaches this acknowledgment nor addresses the specific matters raised will be **noted of
record** as bearing on whether the oath and duties were discharged.

**The Court — oath and canons.** Each judicial officer has sworn: *"{{j_judicial_oath_text}}"*
({{j_judicial_oath}}). Discharging that duty as to this filing means **considering** the specific Rules,
facts, and authorities set out above, stating the legal basis for the ruling ({{j_reasons_for_ruling}}),
and observing the applicable canons of judicial conduct ({{j_canons}}) — impartiality, the right to be
heard, no improper ex parte communication, and disqualification where impartiality might reasonably be
questioned ({{j_disqualification}}).

> ☐ **The Court affirms** that it has considered the matters raised in this filing and has discharged the
> duties of its office with fidelity to the Constitution, the Rules, and the canons of judicial conduct
> ({{j_canons}}) in ruling upon them.
>
> _______________________________, J.    Date: __________________

**Opposing counsel — oath, candor, and the limits of counsel's word.** Counsel of record are **officers
of the court**, sworn to uphold the law, owing a **duty of candor to the tribunal** — counsel shall not
knowingly make a false statement of fact or law to the tribunal, and must correct one ({{j_candor}}).
Counsel is an **advocate, not a fact witness**: a matter of fact is competent only from a witness with
**personal knowledge** ({{j_personal_knowledge}}), and a factual averment is *established* only when
**verified** by a person with such knowledge ({{j_verification}}), subject to the penalties for false
swearing ({{j_unsworn_falsification}}). **Counsel's unverified factual statements are argument, not
evidence,** and a fact not so verified cannot be treated as established.

> ☐ **Counsel affirms**, as an officer of the court, that: (a) counsel's response is made with candor to
> the tribunal ({{j_candor}}); (b) every factual representation therein is either **(i)** made on counsel's
> own **personal knowledge**, or **(ii)** based **only** on information provided to counsel by the client,
> and is not evidence unless verified by a person with personal knowledge; and (c) counsel has made no
> false statement of fact or law to the tribunal and will correct any that appears.
>
> _______________________________    Printed name: ____________________    Date: __________________
>
> Any factual matter relied upon is **verified** by the party with personal knowledge thereof:
>
> _______________________________ (client / affiant), verified subject to {{j_unsworn_falsification}}.

Nothing herein is waived.
