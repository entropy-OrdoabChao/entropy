## PRESERVATION, RECIPROCITY, AND RESERVATION OF RIGHTS

The undersigned **waives nothing, and files this to make that clear of record.** No silence, omission,
or compliance with a court order is, or shall be construed as, a waiver — waiver being the *intentional
relinquishment of a known right* (*Johnson v. Zerbst*, 304 U.S. 458 (1938)).

**A standard the court imposes must apply equally.** Any waiver, deadline, or duty a court enforces
against one party is a rule the court has made; a standard binding one side while excusing the other —
or the court itself — is **wanting in mutuality and therefore void.** There is no such thing as a
unilateral agreement. Equity forbids the asymmetry: **"Equality is equity,"** **"he who seeks equity
must do equity,"** and no party may **approbate and reprobate** — take a rule's benefit while repudiating
its burden.

**Applied to the opposing parties — by the court's own standard and by the Rules.** A party that fails
to answer or respond within the time the Rules allow is subject to **default** ({{j_garnishee_default}}),
and any averment **not specifically denied** in a required responsive pleading is **deemed admitted**
({{j_deemed_admission}}). A party in default cannot obtain affirmative relief.

**Applied to the court — the same reciprocal standard.** The court's duties are mandatory, not
discretionary: it must state the legal basis for its ruling ({{j_reasons_for_ruling}}), honor the oath
of office ({{j_judicial_oath}}), and observe the canons of judicial conduct ({{j_canons}}), including
disqualification where its impartiality might reasonably be questioned ({{j_disqualification}}). Judicial
immunity protects only *judicial acts taken with jurisdiction*; it does **not** reach non-judicial acts
or acts in the **clear absence of all jurisdiction** ({{j_immunity_limits}}), and it does **not** bar
prospective injunctive or declaratory relief.

**Constitutional deprivation — reserved.** Impairing or enjoining a vested right — including the right to
collect a final money judgment — without notice or hearing deprives the party of property, and takes it,
contrary to {{j_due_process_property}}, by conduct under color of law (42 U.S.C. § 1983). The undersigned
reserves this claim and **every remedy not barred by immunity** — declaratory and prospective relief,
recusal, mandamus, appellate review, and referral to the appropriate judicial-conduct authority — and
does not, by filing this, consent to, ratify, or waive any of them.
