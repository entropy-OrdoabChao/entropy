## Financial Inability to Prepay Filing Fees

Plaintiff respectfully submits that the Magistrate Judge's denial of Plaintiff's
application to proceed *in forma pauperis* was erroneous. Under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(a)(1),
a court may authorize the commencement of any action "without prepayment of fees or
security therefor, by a person who submits an affidavit that includes a statement of all
assets" showing inability to pay.

The Supreme Court in *Adkins v. E.I. DuPont de Nemours & Co.*, 335 U.S. 331, 339 (1948),
held that the statute "is designed to ensure that indigent litigants have meaningful
access to the federal courts." An affidavit of poverty is sufficient to establish
eligibility; the statute does not require absolute destitution. *See Deutsch v. United
States*, 67 F.3d 1080, 1084 (3d Cir. 1995) (IFP applicant need not be "absolutely
destitute" to qualify).
