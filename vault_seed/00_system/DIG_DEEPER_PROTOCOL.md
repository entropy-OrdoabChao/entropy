# Dig-Deeper Protocol (user-locked 2026-09-20)

The method, extracted from live sessions (CQV→ch.57, patent→quit-rent, custody→tax): the point
is to "fundamentally understand what is law and not law." Every claim — popular, official, or
theory-layer — gets the same treatment. No claim is dismissed; no claim is adopted; the
governing instrument decides.

## The question sequence (run it on every claim)
1. **"We have X, right?"** — presence check against corpus (identity-tier, not phrasing).
2. **"Where does that COME from?"** — trace to the creating instrument (patent, statute, P.L.,
   charter, signed instrument). Pull the instrument ITSELF, whole — never a summary of it.
3. **"What does it actually say?"** — read the operative words. The instrument routinely says
   something different from BOTH the official gloss (OpenBookPA "becomes property of the State"
   vs enacted "custody and control") and the theory layer (patent = tax-free vs "yielding and
   paying ... for ever").
4. **"Then who owes what, to whom, in what capacity?"** — the money/duty flow. Distinguish legal
   objects that look alike: rent vs tax, custody vs lien, instrument-debt vs statutory levy,
   trustee vs custodian vs fiduciary.
5. **"Doesn't that contradict Y?"** — collision test. Contradictions are FINDINGS: either a
   divergence to expose (state's own gloss vs its statute) or a scope boundary to respect
   (UCC Art 3 reaches instruments, not in-rem levies).
6. **"Something is missing."** — treat the felt gap as real until the sweep proves otherwise;
   user gap-instincts have repeatedly found true holes (pre-1933 public laws, PA per-title
   coverage, SSA manuals).

## Rules the sequence enforces
- Complete instruments, never question-driven slices (title 20 lesson).
- Both-directions verdicts: GROUNDED / DIVERGENT / NOT-IN-CORPUS — applied to the theory layer
  AND the government's own materials with equal force.
- A NOT-IN-CORPUS verdict on a theory claim is paired with the enacted lever that DOES exist
  (patent-exit fails → uniformity/assessment/exemption challenges stand).
- Questions themselves are corpus fodder: record each session's question-chain as learnings so
  the bots run the same sequence unprompted.
