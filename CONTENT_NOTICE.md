# Content notice: what is in this repository, and what is not

## The repository contains software, not law books

Entropy is the software: the engine, the desk, the templates, the skills, and **links** to official sources. It contains no
statutes, no regulations, no opinions, no treatises and no database of any of these. The project's own library (the
database the author built for his own cases) is not in the repository and never will be, because parts of it are
copyrighted works he owns copies of but has no right to hand out. The `.gitignore` refuses databases, chunk stores and model
weights so that cannot happen by accident, and a check before each release confirms the package holds no library data.

## Your library is yours

When you run Entropy it builds a library on your own computer from whatever you give it: the public-domain sources in the
state and federal packs, and anything you own or have access to, including commercial treatises and annotated codes you
bought. What you add, how you use it, what your model quotes from it into your own papers, and whether you share it, are
your decisions under the terms you hold for those works. Entropy imposes no rule on any of it; it never uploads your library,
never sends it to anyone, and never sends a passage to a cloud model unless you pick it and confirm.

## No profit, no servers

Entropy is free software (AGPL-3.0). The project sells nothing, hosts nothing, and has no relationship with any publisher.
Credits for the software it is built on are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
